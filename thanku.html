Thanku

The best part of the camp was meeting him. ❤️‍🩹

I never imagined that, from the very first moment I saw him, he would find such a deep place in my heart. Yet, in those few fleeting days, Thangu became someone we could no longer imagine the camp without. And before we knew it, we began calling him “Thangamaana Payyan.” An authentic boy. A name that seemed to belong to him for sure!!! Thangu turned those days into something beautiful and made the goodbye one of the hardest we’ve ever had to bear. He gave us the brightest parts of himself:) in every memory he created, every quiet effort he made, every moment he shared, and every time he made us feel truly seen, valued, and held close.

He made us feel precious enough to receive a piece of his heart, and I don’t think he will ever know how much that meant to us. Some people walk into our lives for years, while others arrive for only a few days and leave behind a lifetime of laughter and memories. We came to the camp as strangers, but left carrying a little piece of him within us. Maybe that is why goodbye felt so heavy because it was never just a goodbye to a person, but to a feeling, a season, and a small world we had built together. Some people are not meant to stay forever. They are meant to leave light behind. Thangu will always be one of those unforgettable lights. ❤️‍🩹
